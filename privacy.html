Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Greylight is a free photo editing, design and illustration app. This page explains what we keep about you, why, and how to have it removed. In short: we keep only what you type when you sign up and a few facts about your downloads and app use, we never sell it, and you can delete it at any time.

What we keep

  • Your Google account email and name, from signing in with Google. We never see your Google password.
  • What you enter at sign-up: first name, last name, age, country, state or province, and city.
  • Downloads: when you download Greylight, the date, which computer (Mac or Windows), the version and your country.
  • App use: when Greylight opens, it records the date, its version and your computer's system (macOS or Windows).

We don't collect your photos, documents or files. Greylight edits them on your computer and never uploads them. If you send us feedback or a problem report, it goes by your own email and includes only what you choose to send.

Why

  • To let you download and use Greylight with your account.
  • To greet you by your first name in the app.
  • To tell you in the app when a new version is out.
  • To understand how many people use Greylight, where, and on which computers, so we know what to improve.

Who can see it

Only the Greylight team, through a private dashboard. Your details are stored with Google Firebase, which runs the sign-in and the database for us. We don't sell, rent or share your details with anyone else, and we don't use them for advertising.

Age

Greylight accounts are for people aged 13 or older, the same as Google accounts. If you think someone younger has signed up, email us and we'll delete the account.

Your choices

  • See or change your details at any time on My account › Edit my details.
  • Delete your account and details on My account › Delete my account, or by emailing us. Download records stay as anonymous counts.

We keep your details until you delete your account.
