Why Greylight

Everything a photographer and designer needs, in one place.

Most creative work bounces between three apps and three subscriptions. Greylight brings them together and keeps your work on your own computer.

One app instead of three

Develop a photo, take it into a layered design and add vector artwork without exporting and re-importing between programs.

Free, with no subscription

No trial, no adverts and no credit card. Sign up with Google in a few seconds, download it and start editing.

Bring your Lightroom catalogue

Open a Lightroom Classic catalogue and your photos arrive with ratings, flags, labels, crops and Develop settings. Your Lightroom catalogue is only read, never changed.

Fast on your graphics card

Greylight draws with your Mac's or PC's graphics card (Metal or Direct3D 12) and falls back to the processor on computers without one.

Your files stay yours

Photos are edited where they are on your computer and never uploaded. Greylight works offline; only the AI tools you choose to open need the internet.

Hard to lose work

Catalogue edits save as you make them, with automatic backups. If Greylight ever closes unexpectedly, it offers your unsaved work back.

Three workspaces

A full studio, organised the way you work.

Familiar layouts and keyboard shortcuts, so you feel at home from the first minute.

How to install

Install Greylight in a few minutes.

Greylight is free and isn't sold through the App Store or Microsoft Store, so your computer asks you to confirm once, the first time you open it. Watch the short video or follow the steps.

Installing Greylight on a Mac · 49 seconds · no sound
  1. Download Greylight for MacClick Download for Mac and sign up with Google if you haven't yet. The file Greylight-Mac.dmg goes to your Downloads folder. One file works on every Mac, Apple silicon and Intel.
  2. Open the downloaded fileDouble-click the .dmg file in Downloads (or click it in your browser's download list). A window opens with Greylight and your Applications folder.
  3. Drag Greylight into ApplicationsDrag the Greylight icon onto the Applications folder and wait for the copy to finish. You can then eject the disk image (the eject button next to it in Finder's sidebar).
  4. Open GreylightOpen Applications and double-click Greylight. macOS says it can't check it for malicious software. This is normal for free apps not sold by Apple. Click Done.
  5. Allow it once in System SettingsOpen Apple menu › System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway next to “Greylight was blocked”. Enter your Mac password, then click Open Anyway again.
  6. Sign in and you're readyGreylight opens. Click Continue with Google and choose the account you signed up with; Greylight greets you by name. You only do all this once; from now on, open it from Applications, Launchpad or Spotlight (⌘ Space, type Greylight). Tip: keep it in the Dock with right-click › Options › Keep in Dock.

If something looks different

I can't find “Open Anyway”

The button appears in Privacy & Security for about an hour after you try to open Greylight. Double-click Greylight in Applications again, then go straight back to System Settings › Privacy & Security and scroll to the bottom.

I have macOS 12, 13 or 14

You can also right-click (or Control-click) Greylight in Applications, choose Open, then click Open in the message. The System Settings steps above work too.

macOS says the app “is damaged”

This can happen if the download was interrupted. Delete Greylight from Applications and the .dmg file from Downloads, download it again, and repeat the steps.

How do I update or remove Greylight?

To update, download the new version and drag it into Applications, choosing Replace. Your catalogues, settings and files are kept. To remove Greylight, drag it from Applications to the Trash.

Installing Greylight on Windows · 43 seconds · no sound
  1. Download Greylight for WindowsClick Download for Windows and sign up with Google if you haven't yet. The file Greylight-Windows.zip goes to your Downloads folder. If your browser asks, choose Keep.
  2. Extract the zipIn File Explorer › Downloads, right-click the zip and choose Extract All…, then Extract. Greylight won't start from inside the zip, so this step matters.
  3. Move the folder (optional)Move the extracted Greylight-Windows folder anywhere you like, for example Documents. Keep everything inside it together.
  4. Open greylight.exeOpen the Greylight-Windows folder and double-click greylight.exe. Tip: right-click it and choose Pin to Start or Pin to taskbar to open it quickly next time.
  5. Allow it onceIf a blue “Windows protected your PC” message appears, click More info, then Run anyway. This is normal for new free apps; Windows won't ask again.
  6. Sign in and you're readyGreylight opens. Click Continue with Google and choose the account you signed up with; Greylight greets you by name. No installer and nothing else to set up.

If something looks different

My browser warns about the download

Some browsers flag new downloads they haven't seen often. In Microsoft Edge, open the downloads list, click the … next to the file and choose Keep (then Keep anyway). In Chrome, choose Keep.

There's no “Run anyway” button

Click the More info link under the message first; the button appears after it. If your computer is managed by your work or school, they may need to allow the app.

It says a file such as a .dll is missing

Greylight was started from inside the zip, or the folder was split up. Extract the whole zip (step 2) and open greylight.exe from the extracted folder.

How do I update or remove Greylight?

To update, download the new version, extract it and use the new folder; you can delete the old folder. Your catalogues, settings and files are kept. To remove Greylight, delete its folder (and any shortcuts you pinned).

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Write to us; every message is read.

Is Greylight really free?

Yes. The early preview is free to download and use, with no trial period, adverts or credit card. You only need a free Greylight account.

Why do I need an account?

A free Greylight account (Google sign-in) lets the app greet you by name and tell you when a new version is out, and helps us understand who uses Greylight. You sign up once on this site with your name, age and location; when Greylight first opens, you sign in with the same Google account and it stays signed in, also offline. See the privacy policy; you can delete your account any time.

Is it safe? Why does my computer warn me?

Apple and Microsoft show these messages for apps that aren't sold through their stores or signed with a paid certificate, which Greylight, as a free app, doesn't have yet. The message is about where the app came from, not about anything found in it. Download Greylight only from this page.

Will it change my Lightroom catalogue or photos?

No. Greylight reads a copy of your Lightroom Classic catalogue and never writes to it, and your original photo files are never modified. Edits are kept in Greylight's own catalogue. (Lightroom can't open Greylight catalogues.)

Which files can it open?

JPEG, PNG, TIFF, HEIC, WebP, PSD and camera RAW files (for example CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF and DNG) for photos and graphics, and SVG export for vector artwork.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After you've signed in once (that needs the internet), everything runs on your computer and Greylight stays signed in offline. The AI Studio opens AI tools in your browser, which needs the internet.

Is there a version for iPhone, iPad or Android?

Not yet. Greylight starts on Mac and Windows; phones and tablets will follow as the app grows.

How do I get new versions?

Greylight checks for a new version each time it opens and shows it on Home with what's new and a Download update button (also in Help › Check for Updates). Install it the same way as the first time; your catalogues, settings and files are kept. Every version is also listed under Versions.

How do I report a problem or suggest something?

In Greylight, choose Help › Send feedback. It opens your email with the version and device details filled in. Or email greylightcompany53@gmail.com.
